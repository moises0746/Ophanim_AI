## Objective

Define a framework-independent typed policy port, domain policy models, and a safe default-deny engine for task, capability, role, and tool scope without introducing external RBAC providers, secrets, or write execution.

## Scope Delivered

- **Domain Policy Enums & Models** (`ophanim/domain/policy.py`):
  - `PolicyEffect` (`ALLOW`, `DENY`, `REQUIRE_APPROVAL`).
  - `PolicyAction` (`READ`, `EXECUTE`, `WRITE`, `ADMIN`).
  - `PolicyRequest` immutable aggregate validating `subject_id`, `role`, `action`, `resource`, `environment`, `risk_level`, `data_scope`, and `metadata`.
  - `PolicyDecision` immutable result containing `effect`, `reason`, optional `rule_id`, timezone-aware UTC `evaluated_at`, and `obligations`.
  - `PolicyRule` declarative allowlist rule matching on roles, normalized actions, exact/wildcard resources, environments, and maximum risk thresholds.
- **Domain Errors** (`ophanim/domain/errors.py`):
  - `PolicyDeniedError` and `PolicyEvaluationError`.
- **Typed Policy Port** (`ophanim/ports/policy_engine.py`):
  - `PolicyEnginePort` protocol with `evaluate(request: PolicyRequest) -> PolicyDecision` and `enforce(request: PolicyRequest) -> PolicyDecision`.
- **Default-Deny Policy Engine Adapter** (`ophanim/adapters/default_deny_policy.py`):
  - `DefaultDenyPolicyEngine` implementing `PolicyEnginePort`, evaluating requests against allowlist rules and defaulting strictly to `PolicyEffect.DENY` for unmatched or unapproved requests.
- **Unit and Security Tests** (`services/ophanim-core/tests/test_policy_engine.py`):
  - 13 comprehensive test cases verifying domain validation, exact/wildcard matching, environment limits, risk level ceilings, default denial on unconfigured rules, and exception raising on enforcement.

## Architecture Impact

Follows modular monolith boundaries. Domain models and errors remain strictly framework-free and provider-independent (enforced by `test_architecture_boundaries.py`). Policy ports abstract evaluation from any future external policy systems (e.g. OPA, Cedar, or database-driven RBAC).

## Security Impact

Establishes the foundational default-deny security invariant: no agent, task, or capability may execute a tool or access a resource unless an explicit allowlist rule permits it. Production operations, write actions, and higher-risk capabilities default to denial or require human approval.
