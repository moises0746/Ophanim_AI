## Important Product Decisions

- Ophanim AI is a broad AI Coworker platform; the broad vision is not the MVP backlog.
- The Ophanim Assistant is the default human-facing surface, and Ophanim Core remains the control plane.
- The first production vertical slice is the AI Transaction Investigation Agent.
- The initial transaction flow is fully operable through text; voice is deferred product direction.
- The MVP is read-only and produces evidence, classification, findings, limitations, and recommendations only.
- Agent Mesh delegation is bounded, policy-subordinate, and never transfers credentials or authority.
- Activity Feed and Assistant/Agent presentation derive from real sanitized auditable Core events and expose no hidden chain-of-thought.
- Browser use is an optional approved-application fallback in the accepted integration order, not unrestricted automation.
- Database and log retrieval use approved bounded read-only operations; arbitrary SQL and shell are prohibited.
- AnythingLLM is replaceable knowledge, LM Studio is the initial local runtime, Ollama is optional, and Obsidian is human-readable knowledge rather than persistence.
- PostgreSQL is authoritative application persistence and Redis is transient only.
- Future consequential actions remain outside the MVP and require exact human approval plus separately authorized implementation.
