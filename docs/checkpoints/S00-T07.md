## Highest-Risk Threats

- Credential exfiltration through untrusted browser/MCP/knowledge content, prompts, events, logs, screenshots, or transcripts.
- Confused-deputy and privilege expansion through model output, agent delegation, fallback mechanisms, or cross-scope tools.
- Malicious browser/MCP content causing unsafe or cross-domain actions.
- Approval spoofing/replay or stale parameter/precondition reuse.
- Fabricated, replayed, or tampered evidence/events misleading the Activity Feed or audit trail.

## Security Decisions

- Core is the control plane; all model, agent, adapter, browser, MCP, client, and storage paths are subordinate to Core policy.
- Default deny applies to identity, capability, tool, data, environment, domain, application, command, path, visibility, and approval ambiguity.
- Agents own no credentials; tools resolve opaque references only at bounded execution time.
- PostgreSQL is authoritative for canonical state/material events/evidence/audit; Redis is transient.
- AnythingLLM and Obsidian are knowledge boundaries, not workflow/audit persistence.
- The MVP exposes no mutation path; future writes require exact human approval and separate authorization.
- Untrusted content cannot grant authority, and UI projections cannot author events or approvals.
