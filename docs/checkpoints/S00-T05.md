## Key Decisions

- Core owns canonical meaning/state; stable opaque IDs and pinned versions preserve history.
- Delegation, fallbacks, retries, adapters, and approvals cannot widen authority.
- Success requires specified deterministic verification; uncertainty remains visible.
- Evidence provenance remains separate from interpretation/recommendation.
- Large binaries stay outside events/ordinary rows by default; PostgreSQL owns Artifact metadata.
- Approval reserves a safe future record but cannot authorize an MVP write.
