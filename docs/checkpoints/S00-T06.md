## Canonical Decisions

- Core facts and material audit events are authoritative; UI reducers, Activity Feed, Agent Mesh, and animation are projections.
- `occurred_at` and `emitted_at` are distinct; per-task sequence and causation/correlation support ordering and replay.
- `waiting_for_approval` and `error` have precedence over ordinary work presentation; completion requires verification.
- Voice capture/playback events represent real future runtime state; timers may animate within a state only.
- Event payloads are sanitized, scoped, classified, and field-redacted before delivery.
