## Highest-Risk Browser Scenarios

- Malicious page content or deceptive UI induces credential disclosure or a state-changing action.
- Redirect, popup, iframe, custom protocol, or cross-origin transition escapes the approved application.
- AI/vision/coordinate proposal bypasses typed action, policy, or approval boundaries.
- Personal browser profile or auth state leaks credentials across tasks/environments.
- Screenshot, DOM, download, or evidence capture leaks unrelated private data.
- Cancellation or timeout occurs after an ambiguous browser action and the system falsely claims prevention or completion.

## Legacy BrowserUseAgent Gaps

The existing optional `BrowserUseAgent` and `/browser/tasks` endpoint are preserved experimental behavior. Reusable concepts are objective validation, configured domain filtering, max-step limiting, optional dependency handling, and browser cleanup. They lack the authoritative application registry, versioned environment/profile/session model, typed DOM-first action vocabulary, complete navigation and popup/download/clipboard controls, robust state-changing/autosubmit detection, Core event/evidence integration, cancellation reconciliation, full observability, and complete read-only enforcement. They must be wrapped or replaced behind these contracts in a later task and must not be expanded in place.
